# Mave — sourcing research: manufacturers, branding, packaging

Compiled 2026-09-03. Three parallel research passes (China OEM/ODM manufacturers,
Netherlands branding/finishing suppliers, premium packaging suppliers).

**Read this first:** the research environment could not directly open Alibaba,
Made-in-China, Global Sources, or most individual company websites (network
egress was blocked). Every finding below comes from search-engine result
snippets, not verified primary pages. Every company name is **a lead to open
and vet yourself**, not a vetted supplier — confidence levels are noted
throughout. Do not place an order, sign an NDA, or wire money based on this
document alone; see the vetting checklist at the end.

## 2. Netherlands: applying the logo

Two different jobs need two different vendors:

- **Plastic housings (pump, warmer)** → **DeCosmeticaDrukker** (Valkenswaard,
  sister of Artisan Printing Service) — direct printing onto bottles/containers
  of any shape, no stickers, can print on already-finished products.
  **MOQ from 100 units** — the most startup-friendly option found.
- **Stainless steel cooler** → **GTLL Group** (Hengelo) — laser engraving on
  RVS304/316 stainless, the food-grade-appropriate grade for drinkware.
- **Electronics-adjacent housings, tighter tolerances** → **Screen70**
  (Groningen) — pad printing / laser engraving, client base is specifically
  technical/electronics manufacturers.
- Also found: **Artisan Printing Service** (Valkenswaard, 30+ yrs, pad/screen/
  digital printing on all major thermoplastics), **Mevotex** (Enschede, pad
  printing + laser marking under one roof), **Destic Kunststoffen**
  (Veendam/Amsterdam, UV printing + laser cutting + CNC), **Contour Covering
  Technology** (Winterswijk, metal fabrication + UV printing, better suited to
  a full metalworking relationship than pure decoration).

**Recommendation:** brand the pump, warmer and cooler in the Chinese factory's
own tooling as standard production (laser-etch the steel, pad-print or
in-mold the plastics) — a fraction of the per-unit cost of post-import
branding, and one less shipping/handling leg where a finished $100+ unit could
get damaged. Keep a Dutch finisher (DeCosmeticaDrukker especially, given its
100-unit MOQ) as the fallback for pre-MOQ launch batches, limited editions, or
fixing a run that arrives unbranded.

### Recommendation
Run the first launch batch through an NL/EU printer — 2-4 week lead, no
container risk, easy to correct a die-line mistake before committing to a
full China tooling run. Shift bulk reorders to China once per-SKU volume
justifies the MOQ and 6-10 week timeline. For every quote, ask for:

- A structural CAD file **and** a physical structural ("white") sample before
  any print run — test fit, magnet strength, how the product actually sits
  in the box.
- A pre-production printed proof on the final substrate/finish, not a digital
  mockup.
- Pantone (PMS) color matching confirmation and how many correction rounds
  are included.
- The actual **FSC Chain-of-Custody certificate number**, not just a logo
  claim; ask whether soy-based ink is standard or a paid upcharge.
- MOQ per SKU vs. per style (can inserts differ per SKU on a shared shell?),
  and full landed cost broken into unit price + tooling/die charge + freight
  + duties.

## 4. The regulatory finding that changes the timeline

**The breast pump is regulated as a medical device in the EU**, under MDR
(EU) 2017/745 — likely **Class IIa** (a well-supported inference from how
Medela publishes its own MDR QMS certificate, not a directly cited
classification — confirm against Annex VIII or with a regulatory consultant).
The warmer, cooler and storage bags are **not** medical devices; they only
need standard CE (EMC/LVD for the warmer's electronics) and food-contact/
BPA-free/REACH compliance, which moves far faster.

Class IIa in practice requires:
- An **ISO 13485** certified quality management system (yours, or fully
  leveraging the manufacturer's — only Fimilla among the pump suppliers found
  here surfaced this certification).
- A technical documentation file: design history, risk management per
  ISO 14971, clinical evaluation, usability engineering.
- Mandatory conformity assessment audit by an **EU Notified Body** — no
  self-certification, unlike Class I.
- **EUDAMED** registration of the economic operator (SRN), free, ~15-day
  processing.

**Mave, not the Chinese factory, is legally the "manufacturer" under MDR**
once the product is placed on the EU market under the Mave name — this means
Mave carries the QMS, technical file, and post-market surveillance burden
unless a contract specifically transfers obligations. Most premium pump
brands use a regulatory consultancy or a "legal manufacturer" service to hold
the CE certificate and Notified Body relationship rather than build an
ISO 13485 QMS from scratch.

This process realistically runs many months to 1-2 years for a first Class
IIa certificate. **Don't let the pump's regulatory timeline hold back
launching the warmer, cooler and bags** — sequence them independently. The
Netherlands has four MDR-designated Notified Bodies, one of the higher
concentrations in the EU, which is a practical advantage for an NL-based
brand.

## 5. Vetting checklist before committing money

1. **Alibaba Trade Assurance** for the first PO with any new supplier — holds
   payment in escrow until the shipment is confirmed to match spec.
   "Verified/Gold Supplier" badges are self-reported/paid tiers, not
   independent audits — a filter, not proof.
2. **Business license (营业执照)** cross-checked against the National
   Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn) or a paid
   tool (Qichacha/Tianyancha) — confirms the entity is real and currently
   registered.
3. **Third-party factory audit + pre-shipment inspection** — SGS, Bureau
   Veritas, Intertek, TÜV, or QIMA, roughly $300-500/inspector-day. For a
   first order, commission both a pre-production audit and a pre-shipment
   inspection.
4. **Recent, accredited test reports** (SGS/Bureau Veritas/TÜV/Intertek, not
   an unknown local lab) for the specific certifications needed — stronger
   signal than a factory's own claimed cert list.
5. **2-3 rounds of physical samples** before any bulk commitment: initial →
   revised after feedback → pre-production matching final packaging/branding.
6. **Live video factory tour** — walk the actual production floor, ask to see
   the tooling/mold for your specific product, confirm headcount roughly
   matches what's claimed online.
7. **For the pump specifically**, confirm the factory holds or can credibly
   obtain ISO 13485 and has prior experience supporting a Notified Body
   technical file — a materially higher bar than standard consumer
   electronics.
