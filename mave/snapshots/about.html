About Mave

We started with a question: why does the most important equipment a new mother owns look and sound like a medical appliance from 1995?

Mave was founded in Amsterdam in 2023 by a product designer who had just returned to work while exclusively pumping, and a midwife who had watched a thousand mothers give up on pumps that hurt. We spent two years with 400 mothers, two lactation consultants and one acoustics engineer before shipping a single unit.

How we design

Four principles, no exceptions.

01

Numbers, not marketing

'Hospital-grade' means nothing in EU law. We publish suction in mmHg, cycles per minute, decibels and the number of parts you will wash. Compare us on those.

02

Fit first

Half of mothers do not fit the 24 mm flange every other brand ships. Every Mave pump includes six sizes and a 30-day fit guarantee backed by a lactation consultant.

03

Nothing above 40°C

Breastmilk is a living food. Our warmers are physically incapable of overheating it. It cost us eight months of engineering. It was worth it.

04

Designed to disappear

The best product is the one you forget you are using. Silence, few parts, and a shape that fits under a t-shirt are not features. They are the point.

Our promises

What you can hold us to.

100-night trial
Try any Mave pump for 100 nights. Not right? We collect it, sanitise it for our research programme, and refund you in full.
2-year warranty, 3 on motors
Every product, no registration needed. Spare parts are stocked for ten years after a product is discontinued.
Insurance, handled
Most Dutch supplementary insurers reimburse €75–80 towards a pump via a lactation consultant. We pre-fill the form and partner with NVL-affiliated consultants.
Repair before replace
Modular parts, a repair service in Amsterdam, and a take-back scheme. Milk-contact parts are BPA- and BPS-free; steel and glass wherever plastic is not essential.
A human, fast
Email hello@mave.example or chat 08:00–23:00, seven days. Night feeds do not keep office hours, so neither do we.
